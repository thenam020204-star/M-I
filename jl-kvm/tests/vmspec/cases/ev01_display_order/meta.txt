source: jlkvm (this VM, MIDP 2.0 javadoc of Canvas/Display: showNotify before paint, isShown false inside showNotify, repaint coalescing, callSerially after pending repaints); compared with MicroEmulator 2.0.4 and FreeJ2ME-Plus, which differ (below)
diverges: microemu|freej2me
notes: oracle differences. (1) switching canvases: both oracles call showNotify of the new canvas BEFORE hideNotify of the old one, jl-kvm hides first (RI order; unspecified by MIDP). (2) microemu does not coalesce a repaint() that is separated from the next one by a callSerially (paint, R1, paint, R2), jl-kvm and FreeJ2ME do (paint, R1, R2). (3) freej2me keeps only the LAST repaint rectangle (repaint(); repaint(); repaint(5,5,3,3) paints with a partial clip) instead of the union; jl-kvm and microemu paint the union. (4) both oracles call hideNotify when the MIDlet ends; jl-kvm does not (UNVERIFIED on devices). (5) freej2me does not paint a Canvas by itself after showNotify (the test asks for one). (6) the oracles do not change getCurrent() before showNotify; jl-kvm does at once (UNVERIFIED on devices).
